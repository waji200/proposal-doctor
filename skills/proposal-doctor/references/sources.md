# Sources and how far to trust them

Research gathered November 2026. Upwork changes its product often, so re-check anything
that a decision depends on. Nothing here is a controlled experiment.

## Upwork's own material (most reliable for mechanics)

| Source | Used for |
|---|---|
| [Why are my proposals not being viewed?](https://www.upwork.com/resources/why-are-my-proposals-not-being-viewed) | Closest matches shown first; what the client sees before opening |
| [How to boost your proposal](https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-to-boost-proposal) and [Boosted Proposals help](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406541109011-Boosted-Proposals) | Four boosted slots, auction in Connects, placebo jobs, Upwork's own effect claims |
| [Proposal insights](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/34019683309587-Proposal-insights) | Opened, shortlisted and messaged counts per job |
| [Client hire rate](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011238913-What-is-the-hire-rate-I-see-on-a-client-s-profile) | What the hire rate measures |
| [Top reasons you don't get a job](https://www.upwork.com/resources/cant-get-jobs-on-upwork) | Generic and AI-heavy proposals, broad positioning |
| [Availability badge pricing](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/40367930714899-Is-there-a-maximum-price-for-the-availability-badge) | Badge is charged in Connects through the week |
| [Automation policy](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/43342677368467-Use-bots-and-other-automation-properly) | No bulk or automated bidding |

Upwork's figures about its own paid features are marketing claims and differ from page to
page.

## Vendor datasets (large, correlational, conflicted)

| Source | Used for | Caveats |
|---|---|---|
| [GigRadar: reply rate by minutes since posting](https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-outreach) | Timing decay; opener traits of top-quartile bidders; average length 138 words in both top and bottom quartile | 59,339 and 133,872 proposals from the vendor's own customers, Dec 2025 to Feb 2026. Sells auto-bidding. "Reply" is undefined. Some headline claims overstate their own tables. |
| [GigRadar: AI proposals and cliché phrases](https://gigradar.io/blog/ai-proposals-upwork) | Phrase-level reply differences; template engine vs LLM engines; "banana" trap | One-variable comparisons, tiny cells for some phrases, arithmetic slips, same conflict of interest. |
| [Vollna: A/B testing proposals](https://www.vollna.com/blog/a-b-testing-upwork-proposals-the-complete-guide-to-software-and-strategy) | View and reply yardsticks; 60-minute window | Vendor blog. |

Use these for direction ("earlier is better", "stock phrases cost replies"), never for the
exact numbers.

## Writing patterns

| Source | Used for |
|---|---|
| [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) | Catalogue of vocabulary, sentence shapes, formatting and tracking-tag tells, and its warnings about false positives |

That page's own cautions apply here too: no single sign proves anything, people cannot
reliably tell AI from human text, and detectors are unreliable. The voice check is about
what a skimming client skips, not about proving authorship.

## Things often stated as fact that are not established

- An exact preview length. Reports range from about 150 to 225 characters.
- The weights in Best match, or that cover-letter keywords feed the ranking.
- "80 to 90% of proposals are AI." One client's estimate in one forum post.
- Any guaranteed reply rate, best hour of the day, or magic opening line.
